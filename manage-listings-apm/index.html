Tips & how things work
  • Saving changes is the same as publishing. When you click Publish → Publish now, your change goes live within 1–2 minutes.
  • Photos: drag photos straight from your computer or phone into the upload area. Bigger photos (1200×800 or larger) look better.
  • Address autocomplete: start typing the address and pick from the list — the map pin will be set automatically on the next site rebuild.
  • Availability calendar: dates you don't add explicitly default to "inquire only" (safe). Add dates as available to mark them bookable.
  • Stuck? Refresh the page or log out and back in. If something looks broken, text Brad — don't close the tab without saving.
