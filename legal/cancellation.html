Cancellation Policy

We hope your plans don't change. If they do, here's how cancellations work. The exact terms in your signed rental agreement always govern your specific booking; this page summarizes the standard policy.

Standard cancellation timeline

  • More than 60 days before check-in — full refund of all amounts paid, minus a small administrative fee to cover payment processing.
  • 30 to 60 days before check-in — 50% refund of the rental amount. Cleaning fee and security deposit (if held) are refunded in full.
  • Less than 30 days before check-in — no refund of the rental amount. We'll do our best to rebook the dates; if we're successful, we'll refund you the amount we receive from the new booking, minus an administrative fee.

How to cancel

Cancellations must be sent in writing to beachgirloob@gmail.com. The date we receive your written cancellation is what determines the refund tier above. We'll confirm receipt within twenty-four hours.

Cancellations by us

In the unlikely event we have to cancel your stay (a property issue, a safety problem, a circumstance outside our control) we will refund all amounts paid in full and do everything reasonable to help you find alternative accommodations.

Travel insurance

For peace of mind, especially for stays booked far in advance or during peak summer weeks, we recommend looking into travel insurance. Several providers offer trip-cancellation coverage that's inexpensive relative to the cost of a vacation week. We don't sell or endorse a specific provider.

Force majeure

If your travel is prevented by events outside both our control — natural disasters, government-issued travel restrictions, or similar events — we'll work with you in good faith on either a refund or a date change. The specifics will be worked out one-on-one rather than by formula.

Security deposit

If a security deposit is held for your stay, it is fully refunded after checkout once we've confirmed the property is in the same condition you found it. Refund timing and the inspection process are described in your rental agreement.

Questions before you book

Have a question about how cancellations work for a specific date or scenario? Ask before you book — Jill is happy to walk you through it. Email beachgirloob@gmail.com or call (207) 450-7347.
