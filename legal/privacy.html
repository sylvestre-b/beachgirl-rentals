Privacy Policy

We're a small, owner-run vacation rental business. We don't run ads, we don't track you across the internet, and we don't sell your information. This page explains, plainly, what we do collect when you contact us through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Affordable Property Management LLC, operating as Beach Girl Property Rentals. Postal: P.O. Box 254, Old Orchard Beach, Maine 04064. Phone: (207) 450-7347. Email: beachgirloob@gmail.com. The site is operated and managed by Jill, the owner.

What we collect, and why

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (if you give one), the dates and property you're interested in, your guest count, and the message you write. We use this information for one purpose only — to respond to your inquiry, send you a rental agreement if you choose to book, and follow up on the booking itself.

Inquiry messages are delivered to our email through a form-handling service (Netlify or Formspree, depending on routing). They're stored in our email inbox the same way any email is. We do not feed inquiry data into any analytics, advertising, or marketing platform.

Cookies and local storage

We use a very small number of browser-side storage items, all related to making the website work for you:

  • Language preference — if you choose French (FR) or English (EN), we remember it for your next visit. Stored locally in your browser; never sent to us.
  • Cookie acknowledgment — once you dismiss the small notice at the bottom of the page, we remember not to show it again. Stored locally; never sent to us.

Third-party services that load on the site may set their own cookies or transmit your IP address: Google Fonts (typography), OpenStreetMap (the property map), and Netlify (our hosting provider). We do not run advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or social-media pixels.

How long we keep your information

Inquiry emails are kept in our inbox so we can reference past conversations with repeat guests. If you'd like us to delete an inquiry from our records, email us at beachgirloob@gmail.com and we'll confirm when it's done. Once a booking is complete, we keep records of the rental and signed rental agreement for as long as required by Maine state tax and business records law, which is typically up to seven years.

Your rights

Wherever you're contacting us from — Maine, elsewhere in the United States, Quebec, another Canadian province, the European Union — you have the right to ask us what information we hold about you, request a copy of it, request correction of anything incorrect, and request that we delete it (subject to records we're legally required to retain). Email beachgirloob@gmail.com with your request and we'll respond within thirty days.

Children

Our rental services are intended for adults booking on behalf of their families. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under sixteen.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for material changes, post a notice on the homepage.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your information? Email beachgirloob@gmail.com or call (207) 450-7347.

We are not lawyers and this page is not legal advice. It describes our practices in plain English. If you need to verify our compliance with a specific regulation, contact us and we'll do our best to help.
